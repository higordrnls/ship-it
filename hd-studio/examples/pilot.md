# Pilot scenario — custom furniture landing page

Synthetic public example inspired by a generic local furniture studio; not a record about an actual prospect or sale.

## Problem

A local custom furniture business needs a concise mobile-friendly presentation to explain its services and invite prospective customers to request a quote.

## Proposed scope

Hero with clear service promise, service categories, portfolio with approved images (otherwise labeled placeholders), summary of process, contact CTA and verified business details. No ecommerce, online payment, analytics or CRM in the initial scope.

## Open questions

Actual brand assets, confirmed contact destinations, portfolio permission, service area, client approval, price, domain and hosting. No official status is assumed.

## Acceptance

Primary CTA is visible and works for verified destination; before that, preview marks contact as pending. No fictional reviews or claims. The layout is usable at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels, keyboard accessible and free of known critical defects. Preview is unofficial until approved.

## Test of the workflow

Complete brief and visual direction, create the engineering task plan, build and audit in a separate workspace, then prepare an unsent proposal. Measure actual elapsed time and cost privately. No real client identifiers, assets or messages belong in this public example.
