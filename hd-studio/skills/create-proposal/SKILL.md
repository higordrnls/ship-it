# Create proposal

Read HD standards, the brief, audit and any operator-approved pricing. Describe the client's goal, deliverables, exclusions, included revision rounds, assumptions, dependencies, expected milestones, hosting/domain ownership, maintenance options, payment terms and acceptance procedure. Separate one-time costs, recurring fees and third-party charges. Do not invent accepted prices or discounts; flag proposed amounts as estimates and unresolved taxes or fees.

Write a concise client-facing draft at docs/hd/proposal.md or in chat. It must not promise unverified results, guaranteed SEO rankings or untested features. No sending, negotiation or contract acceptance without operator authorization. Handoff upon confirmed client acceptance.
