# Prospect

Read hd-studio/AGENTS.md and STANDARDS.md. Accept a company or public evidence. Check the existing opportunity list before adding another. Inspect relevant public business channels only where accessible. Do not bypass restrictions, scrape personal contacts, or claim exhaustive research.

Record business, available evidence with source/date, observable gaps, likely customer value as a hypothesis, fit for a one-page site, and unknowns. Recommend proceed, hold, or decline with reasons. Draft a short personalized message only if requested; do not send it. Put notes in the private target workspace at docs/hd/opportunity.md, or deliver them in chat if no workspace is available.

Handoff to briefing when the operator selects an opportunity.
