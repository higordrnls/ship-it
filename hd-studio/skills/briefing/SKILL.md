# Briefing

Read hd-studio/AGENTS.md, STANDARDS.md and templates/brief.md. Use the opportunity and existing materials. Distinguish public fact, client-confirmed fact, operator proposal, and unknown. Confirm the main audience, CTA, scope and permissions; ask focused questions only when the answers materially change the design. Never assume an interested prospect approved a purchase.

Write docs/hd/brief.md in the private target or hand off a draft in chat. For an unsolicited preview, proceed with clearly labeled placeholders and unresolved items; do not substitute fabricated content. Handoff to design-direction.
